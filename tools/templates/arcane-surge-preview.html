Arcane Surge — effects preview

Two very different things on one page. The weave is the standing cost — it runs for as long as the party is somewhere unstable — and it is not a shader: it is four SVG paths out of weave-shape.mjs, built by the shipped WeaveRenderer and moved by the suite's own anime.js, against the shipped stylesheet. Nothing about it is reimplemented here, which is the point: a preview built on a second copy flatters whichever copy somebody last touched. It is drawn at the size it ships at, a strip in the time-tracker HUD. The beats below are the real shaders in a real WebGL context, running LIVE — nothing is baked — supersampled and averaged down, which is what keeps the rings and filaments from crawling.
