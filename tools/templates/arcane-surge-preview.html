Arcane Surge — shader preview

The real shaders, compiled in a real WebGL context, driven by the real animation model. Everything here runs LIVE — nothing is baked. The crack row is the standing cost, and it is drawn at the size it actually ships at: a strip in the time-tracker HUD, not a full screen. That size is the whole test — the weave's threads are one device pixel wide and this is the smallest place any effect in the suite has been asked to land. The beats are one-shots; they render supersampled and average down, which is what keeps the rings and filaments from crawling.
