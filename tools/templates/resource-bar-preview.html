Bloodied

Just above and just below half. Every liquid grows slower, paler and gentler, never darker: ink's swirls slow to a quarter and go milky, mercury's sheen slows to a third and fades, lava's pools slow and soften and its glow goes pale.

The surge

The real model, stepped to fixed points after a hit and after a heal and then held. The liquid's texture swings back and forth through the tube and its light lifts, then settles; the edge never moves off the value.

Reactions

The change wave and the impact, in each liquid's idiom and always soft and light: a plume of paler ink, soft mercury ripples and parting droplets, a lava flare with drifting embers.

Lava under a guard break

The lava calms while the guard is broken — its glow evens out, it never darkens — so the initiative tracker's sharp gold fracture reads over it. Ink and mercury beside it for comparison.
